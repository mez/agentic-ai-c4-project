# What are we trying to solve?

The Beaver's Choice Paper Company was losing potential sales due to slow, manual processes for handling customer inquiries, checking inventory, and generating quotes. Basically they don't have enough people to handle the queue of requests that might lead to potential sales!

# How does a human currently handle the process to make sales?

A human would ask the potential customer what they are looking for, answer potential questions and advise on inventory and generate a quote; finally finalizing the sale.

# Abilities they need to have

1. Need to be able to answer potential questions about the product.
2. Need to be able to check inventory of products and order more inventory.
3. Need to understand how to create quotes and apply bulk discounts.
4. Need to be able to make the sale transaction, and advise on delivery timelines.
